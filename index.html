Quick answers.

Which planner should I choose?

Choose the Money Reset System if your main need is organizing paychecks and everyday expenses. Choose the Annual Budget Planner if you want a broader 12-month view with bill, savings and debt tracking.

No. These are digital products sold through Gumroad. Review the product page before purchase for the exact files and delivery details.

Will the planners work with my spreadsheet software?

The products are presented as compatible with Excel and Google Sheets. Check the relevant Gumroad page for specific compatibility requirements before buying.

Do these products provide financial advice?

No. They are budgeting and organization tools, not financial, tax, legal or investment advice. Use your own judgment and consider a qualified professional for advice about your circumstances.
